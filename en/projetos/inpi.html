INPI

I designed the digital experience for INPI's new trademark registration system, integrating artificial intelligence into complex government flows and adapting Brazil's Federal Government Design System.

Aligning AI, government rules, and usability

I joined the product at a critical stage, taking on the Product Design front. The challenge was to turn a lengthy, bureaucratic trademark registration process into a fluid, accessible, and intelligent digital journey.


I structured the flow architecture in Figma, designed the integration of AI assistants into the interface, and created new components while strictly following Brazil's Federal Government Design System (Gov.br).

From concept to in-person sign-off

The development cycle involved continuous cross-functional collaboration and a rigorous approval process with the federal agency's technical teams.

01

Mapping & requirements

I worked alongside the requirements analyst and Product Owners to translate industrial property legislation into clear business rules and screen flows.

02

AI & UI architecture

I designed the interaction components for AI responses, ensuring transparency, predictability, and visual consistency across the system.

03

Refinement cycles

I ran frequent interface validation sessions with POs and developers, adjusting for technical feasibility and adherence to government guidelines.

04

Validation in Rio de Janeiro

I presented the product in person at INPI's headquarters in Rio de Janeiro to a large team of civil servants, led the Q&A session, gathered real-time feedback, and consolidated the final adjustments.

Project Impact

Approved by INPI for public service modernization

The final solution was successfully signed off by INPI's leadership. The work delivered a scale-ready platform, capable of reducing users' cognitive load and speeding up trademark registration analysis in Brazil.
