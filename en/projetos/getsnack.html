B2C Product · SmartStore App Redesign Gamification & FinTech

Getsnack

I redesigned GetSnack's digital ecosystem, unifying the self-checkout purchase journey, digital wallet, and a gamification strategy to increase user retention.

A frictionless journey for everyday consumption

I led the restructuring of the consumer experience in the GetSnack app. The goal was to elevate the brand's visual quality and simplify purchases in unattended smart stores.

I connected different services into a single, intuitive architecture: product browsing, fast checkout, digital wallet balance, and recurrence-based rewards.

Engagement & Retention

GetSnack Club

I conceived the app's gamification system to drive repeat purchases. I ran a competitive benchmark analyzing retention mechanics in major consumer apps, translating best practices into loyalty tiers, cashback, and personalized challenges.


The result was an intuitive structure that balances technical feasibility, fun for the user, and a direct return for the operation.

Design Outcome

A modern experience built for conversion

I delivered a consistent, modular solution that unified the brand's identity and improved purchase completion rates in the app, preparing the product for the store network's future expansion.
