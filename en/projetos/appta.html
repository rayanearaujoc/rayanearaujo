B2B Product · Nutrition Branding Design System

Appta

Appta is a digital platform for nutritionists. When I joined the project, there was no brand, visual identity, or defined structure. The challenge was to build it from scratch, alongside the product itself.

From interview to product

To truly understand who I was designing for, I combined two sources of research: an interview I conducted myself with a reference professional in food consultancy, and recordings of interviews with consultants that the stakeholder had already gathered before I joined.

01

User research

I interviewed a reference professional in food consultancy and reviewed interview recordings with consultants already gathered by the stakeholder.

02

Target audience definition

Based on the insights gathered, I defined more precisely who the platform's target audience would be.

03

AI opportunities

I mapped opportunities for the application, including AI-powered features that could support these professionals' daily work.

04

Collaboration on requirements

I worked closely with a requirements analyst, translating research insights into concrete product specifications.

Before the visuals, the positioning

Based on the insights gathered in research, I developed a branding document, defining the brand's pillars, the personality it should communicate, and the rationale behind the color palette and other visual directions.

This document became the base reference for every decision that came after, from visual identity to the Design System.

A symbol with two meanings

Starting from the approved positioning, I explored different proposals until arriving at the final identity. The symbol is built from the letter "a", referencing both the analysis and organization of data within the platform, and a leaf, connecting the brand to the world of nutrition.

Design System

A foundation to scale the product

With the identity in place, I structured Appta's Design System: typography, colors, buttons, inputs, navigation, component states, and a reusable library, built to speed up the construction of future screens while keeping consistency between them.

Results

From brand strategy to product experience

With the brand, identity, and Design System in place, the project now moves toward building the platform's screens and flows, applying the standards defined throughout the product.
