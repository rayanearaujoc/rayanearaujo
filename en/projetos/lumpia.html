Lumpia

I designed and implemented Lumpia's entire digital experience. From the landing page to the panel used on the wedding day itself, to track guest arrivals in real time.

Presenting the product

I structured Lumpia's landing page: product presentation, key differentiators, how it works, and feature demonstrations, communicating the value proposition clearly for whoever is deciding to hire.

A hub for event management and operations

I developed a complete experience to support organizers before and during the wedding. I built the guest management panel, bringing together confirmations, individual guest information, and the event's key indicators in a single interface, along with a dedicated interface for the wedding day itself, allowing guests to be located and their arrivals tracked and registered in real time.

The solution was designed to simplify an inherently complex operation, with clear, accessible information on both desktop and mobile devices.

Development

From design to code

Beyond designing the interfaces, I was also responsible for the implementation: I built the solutions in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, connected to the back-end, turning UX/UI decisions into a fully functional experience from end to end.

This gave me visibility into the project's real technical constraints, allowing me to structure interactions more precisely and ensure the design intent remained intact through to final delivery.
